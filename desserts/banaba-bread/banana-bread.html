Banana Bread

Adapted from NYT Cooking

Ingredients

Yield: 1 loaf

For the Bread

  • ¼ pound cool butter (1 stick), more for greasing pan
  • ¾ cup dark brown sugar
  • 2 eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 ⅓ cups very ripe bananas (about 5)
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

For the Topping

  • 3 tablespoons chopped walnuts or pecans
  • 1 tablespoon granulated or coarse sugar
  • ⅛ teaspoon cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9-by-5-inch loaf pan. Beat the butter with an electric mixer until light and smooth, then add the brown sugar and keep beating for a couple of minutes until fluffy. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each. Fold in the mashed bananas, stirring just until a few small lumps remain.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Fold the dry mix into the banana batter, stirring only until no dry streaks remain — don't overmix. Pour the batter into the prepared pan. If you're using the topping, combine the nuts, sugar, and cinnamon and scatter evenly over the top.
  3. Bake for 55–65 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Let the loaf rest in the pan for about 10 minutes, then transfer it to a wire rack to cool completely before slicing or wrapping for storage.
